Doorglass Privacy Policy

Ironframe Apps · Effective October 23, 2026

The short version

Doorglass does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Everything it knows stays on your phone.

What Doorglass uses on your phone

  • Usage access. Doorglass reads which app has just come to the front so it can show the lock for the apps you chose. This is checked on your phone, moment to moment, and is never saved or sent anywhere. Doorglass cannot read the contents of other apps — not your messages, photos or screen.
  • Display over other apps. Used only to show the lock screen on top of a locked app.
  • Biometrics. Fingerprint unlock uses Android's system prompt. Doorglass never sees or stores your fingerprint; Android only tells it whether the match succeeded.
  • Your PIN. Stored only as a salted one-way hash in Doorglass's private storage on your phone. The PIN itself is never stored.
  • Your settings and lock list. Kept in Doorglass's private storage on your phone. Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are turned off for this data.

Purchases

The optional one-time unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's Privacy Policy. Doorglass only receives confirmation that the purchase belongs to your Google account, so it can turn on unlimited apps and restore it later.

Network

Doorglass makes no network requests of its own. The only connection is Google Play Billing, used for the purchase and to restore it.

Deleting your data

In Doorglass, open Settings → "Wipe and start over", or uninstall the app. Either removes everything Doorglass stored. There is nothing on any server to delete.

Children

Doorglass is not directed to children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
